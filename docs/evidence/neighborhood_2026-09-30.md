## Validation and corrections

The previous contact-free road artwork sat 11–16 cm above physical terrain. Simply adding real collisions exposed an existing lack of curb traversal: El Recado stalled while walking to the car. Up/forward/down stepping fixes that and separately rejects a 60 cm wall and low ceiling. Replacing the full-height scraping vehicle box with rounded tyre support fixes repeated curb crash damage: the focused full route completes with 990/1000 car health, both walks/drives successful, witnessed incident, 33.3 seconds in pursuit, police within 2.4 m, escape and completed delivery. No pursuit requirements or player health checks were removed.

`surface_contacts.gd` also drives the actual car over a 13 cm fixture curb with 1000/1000 health, verifies road support, all nine vehicle models/seated occupants, bounded chair bullet impulse, submerged-camera environment cleanup and long text containment. The isolated fixture disables geographic water detection outside the map; the real route retains it.

`neighborhood.gd` enters/exits all nine additions using E, uses all six services, walks upstairs into both flats and down again in each residence, rests with health/air restoration and checks wanted restrictions, verifies outfit save/load and gun pose rebinding. An initial duplicated casino partition blocked one residential flat; source furniture/collider metadata removal and relocated kitchen fixed the actual walking path. Record-shop playback now stops on exit, fixing an AudioStreamWAV shutdown leak.

`ballistics.gd` passes penetration, saved glass, wounded armed retaliation, solid player/NPC cover, actual muzzle obstruction, bat cover and 180-shot allocation caps. `police.gd` passes deployment, gunfire, escalation and respray. Existing mission/commerce/casino regressions pass in the complete validator. Final validator/visible performance results are recorded below; after step gating, targeted contacts, police, neighbourhood stairs/services and full routes pass again.

The long route originally emitted six dummy-renderer material errors after pursuit cleanup despite mission success. Weapon model overrides are now shared and retained, reducing per-officer material allocation and preserving lifetime during deferred instance teardown. A dedicated ballistic regression creates visible firearm poses on two officers, adds lit patrols, clears them mid-session and checks shutdown through the validator's strict stderr policy. The final long headless route passes without these errors.

`window_modes.gd` exercises the game's F11 event handler four times on the real GPU, alternating actual 1920×1080 fullscreen and 1280×720 window images. All four transitions pass drawable-size, mode, 85% 3D scaling and HUD enclosure checks, with empty stderr. This regression is included in future `validate.ps1 -Capture` / `-Perf` runs; it was run separately in this session before sustained profiling.
